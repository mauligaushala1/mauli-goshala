🌿 About Our Goshala

Preserving tradition, protecting our sacred cows

Welcome to Mauli Govardhan Goshala

Located in the serene village of Narivali, Wakalan in Thane district, our goshala is dedicated to the protection and care of indigenous Indian cows. We follow traditional Vedic methods to produce the purest and most beneficial cow-based products.

Under the guidance of Pradip Bhoir, we have created a sanctuary where cows are treated with love and respect, and their gifts are processed using time-tested traditional methods.

  • 100% Chemical-Free Products
  • Traditional Vedic Processing Methods
  • Indigenous Indian Cow Breeds
  • Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Practices
  • Direct Home Delivery Available
